Welcome to Dachau

About

Dachau stood as one of Nazi Germany's earliest concentration camps and retained the grim distinction of being the longest-operating one, commencing its operations on the 22nd of March 1933.


This camp was originally established with the purpose of detaining Hitler's political adversaries, encompassing individuals such as communists, social democrats, and other dissenters.


Situated on the site of a disused munitions factory to the northeast of the historic town of Dachau, approximately 16 kilometers (10 miles) northwest of Munich within the state of Bavaria, in southern Germany.


Me and my friend Guilherme decided to save this place for last as it could be considered a "strong" and emotive place to visit, so we waited after completing the exams. It was our very first time in a concentration camp, and its effect was clear. It was very sad to see the conditions the prisioners were held in, especially the ones in the solitary kept by the SD (inteligence police)

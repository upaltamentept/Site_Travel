Welcome to Königssee

About

Located in the Berchtesgaden Alps, specifically in the municipality of Schönau am Königsee, to the south of Berchtesgaden and neighboring the Austrian city of Salzburg, Königssee proudly claims the title of Germany's third-deepest lake.


Renowned for its crystal-clear waters, it boasts the distinction of being heralded as the purest lake in Germany. Thanks to its idyllic location, Königssee and the enchanting parklands that envelop it have become a sought-after destination for both tourists and avid hikers. Moreover, the sheer rock walls encircling the area contribute to the lake's acoustic marvel, renowned for its remarkable clarity.


In 1944, a sub-camp of the Dachau concentration camp was built nearby


Me and my last remaining friends in Germany decided to do this trip as a final goodbye before leaving back home, so we went to the farthest place we could afford, being near the Austrian city of Salzburg.

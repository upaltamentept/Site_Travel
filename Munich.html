Welcome to Munich

About

Munich, or München in German, holds the esteemed position as the capital and most densely populated city within the Free State of Bavaria. As of May 31, 2022, it boasted a population of 1,578,132 residents, securing its rank as the third-largest city in Germany.


In the present day, Munich has evolved into a prominent global hub for various domains, including science, technology, finance, innovation, business, and tourism. This vibrant city has achieved an exceptionally high standard of living, even leading the way in Germany in terms of quality of life.


I choose Munich as my exchange destination due the prestige of the receiving university, the country standing and importance in the world of engineering, and to also get to know a total different culture, a germanic one.

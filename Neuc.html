Welcome to Neuschwanstein

About

Neuschwanstein Castle is a 19th-century palace designed in a historicist style. Perched atop a rugged hill in the foothills of the Alps, it graces the southernmost region of Germany, just north of Austria.


In spite of the Munich Residenz, the extensive palace complex that served as the primary residence for the Bavarian monarchs during that era, King Ludwig II of Bavaria yearned for an escape from what he perceived as the constraints of Munich.

Neuschwanstein embodies the architectural trends of its time, particularly the castle Romanticism, and reflects King Ludwig II's deep admiration for the operas of Richard Wagner


Since I've wanted to visit this castle since my childhood, I reserved to visit it on my birthday, accompanied by my parents
